1. Executive Summary & Paradigm Evolution

Low-Light Image Enhancement (LLIE) has evolved through distinct algorithmic paradigms over the last decade:

  • Phase 1: Classical Statistical & Retinex (Pre-2018): Histogram Equalization (HE, CLAHE), Single-Scale & Multi-Scale Retinex (MSR). Fast but prone to noise amplification, color shifts, and unnatural edge halos.
  • Phase 2: Supervised Deep CNNs & Frequency Decomposition (2018–2020): Retinex-Net, DSLR, KinD. Multi-scale feature extraction on registered paired datasets.
  • Phase 3: Zero-Reference Deep Curve Estimation (2020–2021): Zero-DCE and Zero-DCE++. Formulated enhancement as estimating pixel-wise high-order tone curves using zero paired data via non-reference physical losses.
  • Phase 4: Optimization Unrolling, NAS & Extreme Distillation (2021–2022): RUAS (DARTS architecture search) and SCI / SCI++ (Cascaded self-calibration distilled to a single 3-conv block of ~280 parameters executing in ~5 ms).
  • Phase 5: SNR-Aware Transformers, Learned Priors & Adaptive Dimming (2022–2025): SNR-Net (Transformer + CNN hybrid), CUE (Learnable bilateral & MAE priors), and Dimma (MDN adaptive camera dimmer + diffusion UNet).
Core Engineering Discovery
Model parameter count does not correlate linearly with enhancement performance. SCI (~280 parameters) achieves blazing edge speeds (>200 FPS) while remaining immune to color oversaturation. Modern semi-supervised architectures like Dimma achieve SOTA PSNR (27.39 dB) with only 3 to 8 training pairs by utilizing adaptive camera sensor profiling.

4. Production Deployment Matrix

Edge IoT & Microcontrollers SCI++ / Zero-DCE++

Rationale: Footprint under 10.5 KB (~280 parameters for SCI). Sub-10 ms inference with zero reference data requirements. Fits directly within on-chip cache.

Nighttime Video Surveillance (>60 FPS) SCI++

Rationale: Runs at >200 FPS on GPU. Purely feed-forward 3-conv block ensures zero temporal lag and prevents flickering across changing exposures.

Extreme Darkness & Autonomous Driving SNR-Net

Rationale: Severe low-SNR regions suffer destructive Poisson noise. SNR-Net's Transformer attention synthesizes lost semantics where CNNs output noise.

Computational Photography & Studio Editing CUE / Dimma

Rationale: Highest PSNR/SSIM, learned Bilateral and MAE priors eliminate edge halos, while diffusion-style lightness conditioning produces rich contrast.
